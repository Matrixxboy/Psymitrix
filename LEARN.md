# 🧠 **PsyMitrix — AI-Powered Mental Health Companion**

**PsyMitrix** is an intelligent, accessible, and empathetic AI mental health companion designed to support emotional well-being through modern machine learning and thoughtfully crafted UX.
Built to make mental health care more **personal, data-driven, and supportive**, PsyMitrix integrates reliable assessments, emotional understanding, and wellness tools into a single experience.

---

## 🌟 **Core Features**

### 🤖 **AI Psychiatrist Chat**

A conversational AI trained to provide empathetic, context-aware support.
It uses **sentiment analysis**, **emotion detection**, and **contextual reasoning** to hold meaningful conversations that adapt to the user’s mental state.

### 📊 **Standardized Mental Health Assessments**

Includes clinically recognized screening tools such as:

* **PHQ-9** – Depression Assessment
* **GAD-7** – Anxiety Assessment
* Additional validated assessments for broader mental well-being insights

These tools help users gain structured awareness of their emotional health.

### 🎮 **Interactive Wellness Activities**

Designed to promote daily mental resilience:

* Guided breathing exercises
* Mood and reflection journaling
* Gratitude prompts
* Stress-relief micro-activities

These small habits help users build long-term emotional balance.

### 📈 **Progress & Insights Dashboard**

A clean, intuitive dashboard that visualizes:

* Emotional trends
* Assessment scores
* Daily mood logs
* Chat insights and patterns

Users can track their growth and identify patterns over time.
